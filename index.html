Truss OS

The operating system for an engineering firm

An engineering firm does not run on deals. It runs on sealed sets — where a calc package goes out with an independent check or it does not go out, where the money and the schedule math are the same whether the work is civil, mechanical or software, and where the number that tells you the firm is healthy is a multiplier nobody computes by hand. Truss OS is built around that.

The PE seal gate

Nothing issues for construction until a licensed engineer seals it. Sets ready for the stamp, and sets already sealed, sit on the home screen.

One engine, every discipline

Civil, mechanical or software: the universal core runs the firm and you flip the module. Change the discipline, not the software.

Calcs with an independent check

Calc packages move through QA/QC before they are allowed near a set. The ones waiting on a second pair of eyes are counted every day.

Pursuits with a go/no-go

The pipeline and the hallway argument, replaced by a go/no-go on every pursuit and a fee proposal built before the letter goes out.

RFIs, submittals and permits

Ball-in-court on every RFI and submittal, and every permit in review with the authority, so nothing waits on the engineer unnoticed.

The multipliers, computed

Utilization, net multiplier, realization, backlog, AR and WIP fall out of the same hours and invoices — not reconstructed at quarter end.
